One image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026. A program that writes one image to `/output/entry.png`, built for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

The front page covers six stories: the Shenzhen three-power peace declaration, the ICJ genocide ruling on Myanmar, Russian strikes on Kyiv's bridges, the Houthi missile strike on Riyadh's PIF Tower, Cyclone Akvan's landfall in Odisha, and the global market sell-off — plus a ticker with Kenya's Ebola milestone, the EU–UK reset deal, and COP31. The image is drawn from scratch by `generate_image.py` (PIL) and copied verbatim by `main.ts`.
